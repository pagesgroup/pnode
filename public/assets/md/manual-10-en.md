# Conversion
## Introduction
Use the adjustment list below to convert the machine into another product. All necessary exchange parts are located on or below the surface belts.

> Warning: Conversion may only be made by technically trained personnel familiar with the operation and risks of the machine. This applies to all maintenance and repair work.

## Preparation
Before getting started with the conversion, check that the machine is switched off and locked and that the required tools and parts are within reach.  
  
Follow the steps below so that you do not forget any important adjustments or parts.

## Adjustment list
Numbering and name of adjustment list corresponds with the numbers on the exchange parts and/or numbers provided on the machine. A plastified version of the adjustment list is provided with the machine separately. If applicable.
