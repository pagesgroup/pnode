# Maintenance
## General maintenance points
### Safe maintenance
Only a machine that is properly maintained can continue to work safely and accurately. It is also important to clean the machine regularly.  
  
Carry out maintenance according to the instructions provided below, see also the maintenance schedule in §9.6.

> Warning: Maintenance may only be performed by technically trained personnel familiar with the operation and risks of the machine. This applies to all maintenance and repair work.

> Warning: Always switch off the machine before maintenance. Insert a padlock (ø4-8) in the main switch, keep the key with you and place a warning sign: “Switched off for maintenance", including the name, start and (expected) end date.

### General considerations
- Clean the machine regularly, remove dirt and debris. Keep the surrounding area clean as well.
- Periodically check for damage, loosened fixings or other visual and auditory abnormalities.
- Periodically check the air cylinders and hoses for leaks.
- Keep vacuum ducts and hoses clean.

### Clamp and butt parts
Clean the nipples with compressed air on a weekly basis to remove zinc residues. Every six to eight weeks, remove all nipples and thoroughly clean them. It is important to note that when removing and reinstalling the nipples, ensure that you do not interchange parts that belong together.

## Lubrication
### Linear systems
Proper lubrication is essential to the efficient performance of the linear system. Improper lubrication will result not only in increased risk of wear, but also in significant shortening of the service life.  
  
Lubrication:
- Reduces wear and friction resistance, such as internal erosion of the moving parts.
- Creates an even coating of grease on the conveyor belts, reducing the load pressure and extending the service life.
- Protects metal surfaces against corrosion.

To avoid compromising the linear system's performance and maintaining the lubrication over a prolonged period of time, the lubricant used must be compatible with the ambient conditions and meet all specific requirements. 
  
In general, a lubrication interval of 5000 km operating capacity or a minimum of once per 6 months must be observed. This capacity may be lower under special ambient conditions. Relevant factors may include:
- Extreme operating temperatures;
- Influence of condensation and splash water;
- High vibration load;
- Deposit from certain substances (acids, hydrocarbons);
- High dynamic use;
- Permanently small stroke movements.

The lubrication of the linear system is done using the grease nipple on the linear carriage and a suitable grease gun. Make sure to use a grease gun with a known discharge rate (such as THK MG70) and the appropriate type of grease. Please be aware that excessive grease can lead to excessive pressure in the bearing block, which can cause premature failure. Refer to the relubrication table for the recommended amount and consult the maintenance schedule for the appropriate intervals.

> Warning: Grease Type: Lithium based EP2 grease (for example THK AFB-LF Grease).  
Too much grease can cause premature failure!

## Timing belts
The machine is equipped with timing belts for performing a variety of movements. The timing belt drives must be regularly inspected.
  
![Timing-belts-1](assets/image/manual-timing-belts-01.png)

### Checklist for maintenance of timing belts
- Switch off the drive.
- Place all machine parts in a safe (neutral) position
- Check the belt for wear or damage. Replace if necessary. See Installation of timing belts.
- Check the disks and gears and replace if worn or damaged.
- Also check other drive components such as bearings, shafts, mounting points, etc.
- Check belt tension and adjust if necessary.
- Recheck alignment of disks.
- Connect the machine and let the drive run. Look and listen for abnormalities.

### Installation of timing belts
1. After switching off the machine and locking the main switch, disconnect the attachment points in the motor or the tensioner. Remove the belt without bending or twisting it. Do not twist a belt off the disk!
1. Remove and inspect the old belts. Abnormal or excessive wear may indicate improper maintenance.
1. Choose the proper replacement belt. (Refer to the mechanical parts list).
1. Clean the disks and tensioners with a cloth that is slightly moistened with a mild, non-volatile solvent. Do not use a sharp object to remove grease or dirt from the belt. Belts must be fitted dry onto the drive.
1. Check the gears for extraordinary or excessive wear. Also check the alignment. Correct alignment is particularly critical in synchronous belts.
1. Check the other drive components. Are bearings and shafts properly aligned, well lubricated? Are they worn out?
1. Install the new belt on the gears. Do not twist or use force to mount it.
1. Adjust the belt until the tension gauge shows the correct tension. Turn the drives a few revolutions by hand and check the tension again.
1. Properly tighten the bolts of the mounting points in the motor or the tensioner. All drive components must be firmly secured in place because any change to the centre distance compromises the drive's performance.
1. Although the belts do not require re-tensioning, we recommend that you let the drive run and observe it carefully. Look and listen for unusual noises and vibrations. Switch off the machine and check the bearings and motor. If they feel hot to the touch, the belt may be too tight, or the bearings may be misaligned or improperly lubricated.{{/timingBelts}}

## Hydraulic aggregate
### Visual inspection
A visual inspection of the machine must be performed when starting the machine, or at least once a day, to identify any obvious shortcomings:
- Illegible directions or warning signs
- Leaks
- Loose and/or missing parts
- Signs of corrosion
- Signs of external impact

### Inspection of the accumulator
See maintenance table for frequency of inspection.
The pre-pressure of the battery is 90bar.
The general rule is that if the pressure difference increases plus or minus 5 bar compared to the standard pressure of 90 bar, then the battery must be recharged with a pressure of 90 bar. Keep the following points in mind before you start the inspection.
- The system must be switched off.
- Inspect only without hydraulic pressure.
- Inspect preferably at the environment temperature (the pre-pressure is measured at a temperature of  20 degrees). When the environ temperature is higher, it’s possible that a higher pre-pressure is measured.

> Warning: A higher pressure can be the result of a higher temperature in the battery. In that case you will have to wait until the temperature in the battery equals the environment temperature.

Lower nitrogen pressure may be due to:
1. leaking nitrogen valve.
1. the normal use of the machine (a pressure loss of 10% per year can be considered normal depending on the working pressure, the pre-pressure, the load, the load etc.).
1. a leaking membrane.
1. a lower temperature in the battery (less than 20°c).

### Check the filter elements for pollution
![Aggregate-check-filter-elements-1](asssets/image/manual-aggregate-check-filter-elements-01.png)  
  
The filter must be checked daily for pollution. Bosch Rexroth filters are normally used. If the permissible thrust / differential pressure for these filters is exceeded, an optical signal is given. A red pin appears.  
After a cold start, press the red pin of the pollution indicator after the operating temperature has been reached (check function). If the pin immediately returns with this check, the filter element must be replaced by the end of the shift at the latest.

### Fluid level
The level of the hydraulic oil must be checked regularly to ensure proper operation and to prevent wear of the hydraulic system. The oil level is not constant while the hydraulic unit is in operation. Level changes arise due to different volume requirements for oil intake/delivery at hydraulic cylinders and the battery during a work cycle. When visually inspecting the oil level, observe a complete work cycle of the machine for the reasons stated above, to determine whether the oil needs to be topped up and by how much. The oil level must not exceed the upper mark during operation and must not fall below the lower mark. If the minimum fluid level is exceeded, there is a risk that the pump may stop due to cavitation. If the maximum fluid level is exceeded, it could lead to heat expansion of the oil or water penetration (e.g. water in the event of internal leaks of the oil-water heat exchanger).

> Warning: Note: no hydraulic oil should be lost with use of the machine, if this is the case, there is a leak.

### Changing the hydraulic oil
All hydraulic contained oil in the system must be changed every 5 years to maintain correct operation and to prevent wear. The oil must be completely drained from the system when replacing the oil. Afterwards, the system must be refilled with a suitable medium up to the correct level.

> Warning: When changing the oil, the same type of oil must be used as the oil that is currently used in the machine. Standard hydraulic oil used is Renolin B10VG32.

### Decommissioning
Prepare containers that are large enough to absorb all of the oil. The entire volume of the hydraulic system consists of the volumes in the tank, the piping system, the drives, etc.
- Disable the machine as described in paragraph 5.3.
- Switch the hydraulic pressure system and lock it.
- Release the pressure on the oil side of the battery.
- Allow the oil to drain into the containers. Make sure that the pipes and consumers are completely drained. Perform any venting measures that may be required.

### Maintenance and repairs
**In the event of leaks at the couplings**, tighten them by one rotation. Make a note of the leaks. If leaks continue at this coupling, the coupling must be thoroughly examined to identify the cause of the leak. Depending on the findings, the seals must be replaced and/or the coupling and pipe must be replaced. In the event of leaks on flanges, the same procedure must be followed.  
  
**With cracks or leaks on the welding seam** the cause must be identified and resolved. The relevant components must then be replaced or repaired professionally.  
  
**If there are signs of external impact** the cause must be identified and resolved. The component and the adjacent pressure-retaining components must then also be checked for damage and assessed for continued reliable use. If necessary, these parts must be replaced or repaired professionally.  
  
**If there are signs of corrosion** the component must be checked for damage and assessed for continued reliable use. If necessary, this component must be replaced or repaired professionally. Corrosion protection must be applied in any event.  
  
**Hydraulic medium**:  Mineral oil DIN 51524 / ISO VG 32  
  
Examples of suitable hydraulic oil:
- Fuchs Renolin B10VG32
- Shell Tellus S2 M32
- Castrol Hyspin AWS32
